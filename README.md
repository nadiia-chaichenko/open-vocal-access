Open Vocal Access

An open-source initiative exploring accessible classical vocal repertoire, multilingual music education and digital tools for singers.

Mission

To make specialist classical vocal resources more accessible to students, independent performers, educators and researchers.

Project Focus

* Classical vocal repertoire discovery
* Multilingual pronunciation and text study
* Accessible educational resources
* Voice-type-specific repertoire exploration
* Open-source music technology

Development

This project is in its early development stage. Initial resources and tools are being prepared for public use and collaboration.

About

Founded by Nadiia Chaichenko, a London-based classical vocalist studying at the Royal College of Music.

The project brings together professional musical expertise, accessibility and emerging digital technologies.
